About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public research university in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia, specializing in marine science, oceanography, fisheries, and aquatic resource management.

History: Started in 1979 as a small fisheries center. After a few name changes, it became an official university called Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) in 2007.
