About the Event

The Programming Workshop 2026 is a one-day event organised by the Faculty of Computing to help students improve their programming skills, explore new technologies and learn from experienced speakers.

Speaker 1: Dr. Ahmad Rahman

Dr. Ahmad Rahman is a software engineer and lecturer specialising in web development. He will introduce students to HTML fundamentals and website creation.

Speaker 2: Ms. Nur Aina

Ms. Nur Aina is an experienced application developer. She will conduct a hands-on programming session where students can practise problem-solving and coding.
